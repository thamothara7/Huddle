# Huddle — Terms & Conditions

_Last updated: 2026-05-25_

## 1. About this app

Huddle is a moderation tool for subreddit moderators, built on Reddit's Devvit Developer Platform. The source code is open source under the [MIT License](https://github.com/thamothara7/Huddle/blob/main/LICENSE). These Terms govern your use of the deployed Huddle app inside a subreddit you moderate.

## 2. Who may use Huddle

Huddle is intended for installation by subreddit **moderators** inside subreddits they moderate. Reddit's Devvit installation flow gates this — only moderators of a subreddit may install an app there.

By installing Huddle, you confirm that:
- You are a moderator of the subreddit where you are installing it.
- You agree to use Huddle in accordance with [Reddit's Content Policy](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/content-policy), [Reddit's Moderator Code of Conduct](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/moderator-code-of-conduct), and [Reddit's Developer Terms](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/developer-terms).

## 3. What Huddle does and does not do

Huddle surfaces context (factual user activity summaries, mod-action history, AI-suggested moderation actions) to assist moderators in making decisions inside the modqueue. Huddle **never takes a moderation action without an explicit click from a moderator**: there are no autonomous bans, removes, approvals, or spam-marks.

AI features (summaries, suggested actions, removal-reason drafts) are advisory only. The moderator remains the decision-maker and is fully responsible for every action they take through Huddle.

## 4. Acceptable use

You may not use Huddle to:
- Harass, defame, or selectively target individual users or groups in violation of Reddit's policies.
- Circumvent moderation decisions made by other moderators of the same subreddit.
- Mass-action items in a way that violates Reddit's API rate limits or anti-spam policies.
- Re-host, mirror, or redistribute the Huddle source code in violation of its [MIT License](https://github.com/thamothara7/Huddle/blob/main/LICENSE).

## 5. AI features and accuracy

Huddle's AI surfaces (summary, suggested action, draft removal reason) are generated by Google Gemini Flash. They are based **only** on a structured fact dict — never on the content of the reported item. Output can still be inaccurate, incomplete, or context-blind. You agree to:

- Verify AI suggestions against the underlying facts (visible in the Context Peek drawer) before acting.
- Edit AI-drafted removal reasons before posting if anything is unclear or wrong.
- Not rely on Huddle's AI surfaces as the sole basis for any consequential moderation decision.

## 6. Privacy

Data handling is described in detail in the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/thamothara7/Huddle/blob/main/PRIVACY.md). In summary: Huddle reads report metadata via Reddit's Devvit triggers, stores per-user activity facts in subreddit-scoped Redis, and sends a fact dict (never the content) to Google Gemini for the AI surfaces.
